# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project welcoming and free from harassment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respectful disagreement, and attention to the community's well-being. Unacceptable behavior includes personal attacks, discrimination, harassment, threats, and revealing someone else's private information without permission.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove or edit inappropriate contributions and restrict participation when necessary.

## Scope

These standards apply in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other public settings where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to a project maintainer through the repository's available contact mechanism. Maintainers will review reports fairly, respect confidentiality as far as practicable, and take appropriate action.

## Enforcement Guidelines

1. **Correction:** For a minor first incident, provide a private written warning and ask for the behavior to stop.
2. **Warning:** For repeated or more serious behavior, issue a formal warning with stated consequences.
3. **Temporary restriction:** For sustained unacceptable behavior, temporarily limit participation.
4. **Permanent restriction:** For severe or repeated violations, permanently prohibit participation.
