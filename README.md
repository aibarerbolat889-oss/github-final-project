# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple Bash calculator that computes simple interest from the principal amount, the annual interest rate, and the time period.

## Formula

**Simple Interest = (P × R × T) / 100**

- **P (principal):** initial amount of money
- **R (annual interest rate):** percentage per year, for example `5` for 5%
- **T (time period):** number of years

## Example

For a principal of 1000, an annual interest rate of 5%, and a time of 2 years:

`(1000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = 100`

The simple interest is **100.00**.

## Requirements and usage

Bash and awk (available on Linux, macOS, or Git Bash on Windows).

```bash
bash simple-interest.sh
```

Enter the principal, interest rate, and number of years when prompted.
